# MotoBays Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 October 2026
**Applies to:** the MotoBays Android app (`uk.motobays.app`) and the pages on this site

## 1. Who we are

MotoBays is a free app that helps motorcyclists find parking bays in the United Kingdom. It is operated by the MotoBays developer (the "data controller" under the UK GDPR).

**Contact:** dwayne.edwards89@googlemail.com

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your personal data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

## 2. Summary

- You can use the app without an account. Browsing bays, searching and getting directions involve no account and no data about you other than what the services in section 6 receive to answer a request.
- The app only uses your location when you tap the "my location" button, and never stores or uploads it.
- If you sign in (Google or Apple) and post photos, comments or bay changes, we store those together with your account ID so they can be reviewed and shown to other riders.
- There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
- You can delete your account and everything linked to it from inside the app at any time.

## 3. Personal data we collect

| Category | Data | When we collect it |
|---|---|---|
| **Account** | A unique user ID, your email address, whether the address is verified, and which provider you used (Google or Apple) | When you sign in |
| **Profile** | The display name you choose | When you save a display name |
| **Content you post** | Photos, comments, new bays and corrections to existing bays, each with the date, time and your user ID | When you submit them |
| **Reports** | The item reported, the reason you pick, any note you add, and your user ID | When you report another rider's content |
| **Location** | Your device's current position (precise or approximate, your choice) | Only while you use the "my location" button or search with location allowed. It stays on the device and is never sent to us. |
| **Device data on request** | Device model, Android version, app version and a technical trace | Only if the app closes unexpectedly and you choose to send us the report by email |
| **Stored on your phone only** | Saved bays, settings, blocked riders, recent searches, last map position | As you use the app. This data never leaves your device except through Android's own backup, if you have it enabled. |

We do not collect contacts, your photo library, your files, advertising identifiers, or any analytics about how you use the app. When you add a photo, Android's photo picker or camera hands us only the picture you chose, and we re-encode it before upload, which removes location and camera metadata.

## 4. Why we use it and our legal basis

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (UK GDPR Article 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Show you the map near you and distances to bays | Location | Your consent, given through the Android permission prompt. You can withdraw it in Android settings at any time. |
| Run your account and show your posts with your display name | Account, profile, content | Performance of a contract (providing the service you asked for) |
| Review posts before they are published, act on reports, and let riders block each other | Content, reports, account | Our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and accurate, and in meeting Google Play's content policies |
| Stop automated abuse of our backend | Device attestation token (Firebase App Check) | Our legitimate interest in protecting the service |
| Fix crashes | Device data, only when you send it | Your consent, each time you tap Send |
| Respond to your rights requests and legal obligations | Account, email | Legal obligation |

## 5. Who can see your content

- **Approved** photos, comments and bay changes are public: anyone using the app can see them, with your display name (or "Anonymous rider" if you have not set one).
- **Pending and rejected** items are visible only to you and the moderator.
- Your **email address** is never shown to other riders. Only the moderator can see it.
- **Reports** are seen only by the moderator. The person you report is not told who reported them.

## 6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers. The app relies on these service providers:

| Provider | What they process | Role and location |
|---|---|---|
| **Google LLC – Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, App Check) | Account, profile, content, reports, attestation tokens | Our processor. Firestore data is stored in Google's EU multi-region (`eur3`); Firebase Authentication is a global service. Google's commitments: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy |
| **OpenFreeMap** | The map tiles you request (which reveal the area you are looking at) and your IP address | Independent provider of map tiles: https://openfreemap.org |
| **Photon by komoot** | The text you type into place search and your IP address | Independent provider of place search: https://photon.komoot.io |
| **postcodes.io** | Postcodes you type and your IP address | Independent provider of postcode lookup: https://postcodes.io |
| **GitHub** | A request for the bay data file and your IP address | Hosts the bay data file: https://docs.github.com/site-policy |

Requests to these services carry the app's name and version so they can identify traffic. None of them receive your account details, your posts or your location fix.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, for example in response to a valid request from a UK authority.

## 7. International transfers

Google may process Firebase data outside the UK, including in the United States. Transfers are covered by the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and Google's Data Processing Terms. The map, search and postcode providers receive only the request data described above.

## 8. How long we keep data

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and profile | Until you delete your account |
| Posts | Until you delete them, the moderator removes them, or you delete your account |
| Reports | Up to 12 months after they are resolved, then deleted; unlinked from your account immediately if you delete it |
| Location | Never stored |
| Crash reports you email us | Until the bug is fixed, then deleted |
| Google backups of Firebase data | Purged within 180 days of deletion |

## 9. Security

All traffic between the app and our services uses HTTPS. Access to data in Firebase is controlled by security rules that are tested before every release: riders can only read approved content and their own items, and only write to their own account. Firebase App Check ensures only genuine copies of the app can talk to the backend. Photos are size-limited and re-encoded on the device before upload.

## 10. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask us to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** it;
- **delete** it (see section 11);
- **restrict** or **object** to our processing;
- **port** it to another service in a machine-readable form;
- **withdraw consent** where consent is the basis (location, crash reports).

You can do most of this yourself: **Settings › My submissions** shows everything you have posted and its status, and **Settings › Account › Delete account & data** removes all of it. For anything else, email dwayne.edwards89@googlemail.com from the address linked to your account. We respond within one month and never charge a fee for a reasonable request.

## 11. Deleting your account

Open **Settings › Account › Delete account & data** in the app, or follow the instructions at [Delete your MotoBays account](delete-account.html). Deletion removes your sign-in, your display name, and every photo, comment and bay change you submitted. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

## 12. Children

MotoBays is intended for riders aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16; if you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your data, the app will tell you the next time you open it.
