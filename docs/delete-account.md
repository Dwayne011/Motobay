# Delete your MotoBays account

**App:** MotoBays (`uk.motobays.app`) · **Developer contact:** dwayne.edwards89@googlemail.com

You can delete your account and everything linked to it yourself, in the app, in under a minute. If you no longer have the app, email us and we will do it for you.

## In the app (immediate)

1. Open MotoBays and go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account & data**.
3. Confirm with **Delete**.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, that is Firebase's safety check for recent sign-in: tap **Sign out**, sign in again, then repeat the steps.

## By email

Send an email to **dwayne.edwards89@googlemail.com** from the address you signed in with, with the subject **"Delete my MotoBays account"**. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by reply. We only use the email to verify it is your account.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in (Firebase Authentication user) and the email address linked to it
- Your display name
- Every photo you uploaded, including the image files
- Every comment you posted
- Every bay you added and every correction you suggested, whether approved or not
- Reports you made are kept but no longer linked to an account

Deletion is permanent and takes effect immediately in the app; Google's backups of the Firebase data are purged within 180 days.

## What is kept

- Saved bays, settings and blocked riders on your phone are not touched by account deletion. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes them.
- Nothing else: the app holds no other data about you. See the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

## Partial deletion

To remove a single photo, comment or bay change without closing your account, ask by email with a link or description of the item, or wait for it to be reviewed: anything not yet approved can be withdrawn on request.
